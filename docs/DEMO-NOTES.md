# Demo notes: the moments worth filming

The hackathon asks for a demo under 90 seconds that shows the plugin working. Judges weigh usefulness, reliability, polish and creativity, and favor plugins that do more than a prompt or a skill could. So the film should show, in this order: a place you want to write in, help that runs with no model at all, the editor's notes landing on exact words, and the writer staying in charge of every word.

## Seeded data

- **The piece:** the sample that ships with the plugin, "The room nobody counts" (343 words, a short essay about a branch library facing cuts). It is The Clearing's own text. It has no client material and nothing about the contest.
- **Fresh start:** film in a fresh Hermes profile, so the sample opens on its own with the invitation note. If the invitation has already been dismissed, start at step 2 with Cmd E and "quick" instead.
- **Reset between takes:** title menu, **History**, restore **First words**. Restoring keeps the edited text as a version, so nothing is lost.
- **Model:** pick a fast model for The Clearing editor (Settings, Model, Auxiliary models) and run every model step once before recording, so you know its timing. Cut the wait in editing; leave one second of "Reading your piece" with its counter so viewers see it is live.
- **Window:** Hermes at about 1400 x 860, light mode, default accent. The room's layout is tuned for a pane 1,100 px or wider.

## The shot list (about 85 seconds)

| Time | Moment | Exact steps | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:06 | The room | Click **The Clearing** in the sidebar. | A winter lake, a page in Caslon, one quiet note in the margin offering a check that runs on this computer. |
| 0:06 to 0:17 | Quick check, no model | Click **Quick check**. On the first note ("It is important to note that"), click **Fix it myself**: the cursor lands at the start of the phrase. Hold Shift and press Option+Right six times, then Delete. Click **Leave it** on the next. | Five underlines appear at once. The first note opens with what it found and "These are questions, not rules." The note is pinned to its words. When you delete the phrase, its mark goes with it. |
| 0:17 to 0:28 | Better words, no model | Double-click **modest** in "a modest efficiency". Click **small**. Then click **modest** in "Words you tried". | Meanings and other words in the margin. The word swaps in place with a wash, and the original is one click away. |
| 0:28 to 0:44 | The Ask line and your model | Press **Cmd E**. Type "where do readers get lost". Press **Enter**. | The library answers plain words. "Reading your piece" with a counter, then a note naming the pass and your model, ending in a question. |
| 0:44 to 0:58 | Openings in place | Cmd E, "openings", Enter. Click **A**, then **B**, then **Yours**. | Three new openings appear in the page one at a time, in sans so it is clear they are not yet yours. **Yours** brings your own back. |
| 0:58 to 1:10 | A shorter draft | Cmd E, "shorter", Enter. Click one struck passage to keep it. | Passages struck through in place, a word count before and after, and a cut you click comes back. |
| 1:10 to 1:17 | Night | Switch Hermes to dark mode. | The lake turns to a night shore. Same page, same notes. |
| 1:17 to 1:27 | Finish | Click **Finish**, then **Save as Word**. | Hermes's own save dialog with "The room nobody counts.docx". |

End on the page, not on a dialog. Close the save dialog and hold for one second on the room.

## Backup moments (if a model step misbehaves on the day)

- **No model, handled honestly.** In a profile with no model: Cmd E, "weak sentences", Enter. "Hermes doesn't have a model ready for The Clearing" with **Open Settings**, and the draft untouched. Useful for a reliability cut.
- **Find weak sentences.** Cmd E, "weak", Enter. Notes walk one at a time with **1 of 3**; the page scrolls to each passage.
- **Fix punctuation and typos.** Change records with **Keep** and **Skip**, then **Apply**: shows the model never edits without a yes. Typing "spelling" or "grammar" on the Ask line finds it too.
- **A novelist's Ask line.** Cmd E, "fiction": the storytelling techniques, each labeled "Find the human story · fiction, memoir". Good for a creativity cut.
- **A reporter's draft.** Paste a piece with quotes and run Quick check: it leaves the quotes alone and says so on its first note.

## Lines that are true and short, for a voiceover or captions

- "A writing room inside Hermes."
- "Quick check and the thesaurus run on this computer. No model."
- "Every note is pinned to your exact words."
- "Your model edits. It never rewrites."
- "You keep or skip every change."

## Avoid on camera

- Hermes's settings pages with any account, key or provider name visible.
- Any real draft with names of clients or the contest.
- A model name you don't want shown: the note's top line names the model.
